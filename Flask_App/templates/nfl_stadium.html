How these rankings work

Same team, venue, and season. Only completed regular-season and playoff games count. A section needs at least 3 comparable games and coverage of at least 60% of completed games in this report.

Cheapest: every included game must cover all three final-day windows (12–24, 6–12, and 0–6 hours). Window medians are weighted by duration, then game averages receive equal weight. No missing prices are filled in.

Drops: all five windows in the final {{ '48 hours' if sport_key == 'mlb' else '7 days' }} must have at least three observations. We find each game's largest earlier-high to later-low decline, then take the median across games. These are observed price histories, not guaranteed savings.

Sparse sections remain available below, but cannot win a ranking based on a single cheap game. These coverage rules are safeguards, not statistical confidence intervals.
